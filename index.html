Verra Mobility

Operator Reporting Workflow Automation

Problem: Stakeholders had to spend hours pulling operator-specific data manually, combining files, and navigating a complex reporting process before they could review performance.
Tools: Power Automate, Outlook, SharePoint, Power BI, scheduled refreshes.
Solution: Created an automated workflow that captured operator data through Outlook, stored it in SharePoint, refreshed it daily, and connected SharePoint to Power BI so stakeholders could review everything in one central dashboard.
